Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 November 2026

1. Data We Collect

When you create an account, we collect your name, email address and password (stored in hashed form). When you use Spendly, we collect the expense records you enter, such as amount, category, date and description.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use your information to sign you in, store your expenses, and show you your own summaries and spending breakdowns. We do not sell your personal data, and we do not use your expense records for advertising.

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored in the Spendly database. We keep it for as long as your account is active. You can delete individual expenses at any time, and you can ask us to delete your account and the data linked to it.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly loads fonts from Google Fonts, which may log basic request information such as your IP address. We do not share your expense records with other third parties. If we add a service that needs your data, we will update this policy first.

5. Cookies

Spendly uses cookies only to keep you signed in. We do not use cookies for tracking or advertising.

6. Your Choices

You can view, edit or delete your expenses at any time. You can ask us for a copy of your data or for your account to be closed by contacting us.
